# Third-party components

Fenrir bundles or depends on the following third-party projects.

- **CachyOS-Live-ISO** (this repo's base): GPL-2.0-or-later, from CachyOS.
- **Caelestia dotfiles** (embedded under `archiso/airootfs/etc/skel/.local/share/caelestia`,
  and `caelestia-cli`/`caelestia-shell`/`caelestia-meta` built by `build-local-repo.sh`):
  GPL-3.0-only, from https://github.com/caelestia-dots.
- **CachyOS packages** (Hyprland stack, Calamares, cachyos-fish-config, etc.):
  various licenses, see each package's own upstream repository.
